Subject–Verb
Agreement

Subject ও Verb-এর মধ্যে সংখ্যাগত সামঞ্জস্যই হলো Subject–Verb Agreement। ইংরেজিতে সঠিক বাক্য লেখার জন্য এটি অত্যন্ত গুরুত্বপূর্ণ একটি grammar rule।

Singular Subject → Singular Verb
Plural Subject → Plural Verb
🎯 মূল ধারণা

Singular Subject

He plays football.
Rahim goes to school.

Plural Subject

They play football.
The boys go to school.

Golden Formula

একবচন Subject হলে Singular Verb এবং বহুবচন Subject হলে Plural Verb ব্যবহার করতে হবে।

SUBJECT
SINGULAR → SINGULAR VERB
PLURAL → PLURAL VERB
১–৫. মৌলিক নিয়ম
1 Singular Noun → Singular Verb
The boy plays football.
Rahim goes to school.
2 Plural Noun → Plural Verb
The boys play football.
They go to school.
3 I-এর বিশেষ নিয়ম
I go to school.
I play cricket.
I have a book.
4 You → Plural Verb
You are a student.
You have a pen.
5 Be / Have / Do
Subject Be Have Do
He/She/It is has does
I am have do
You/We/They are have do
৬–১০. And, Each, Every
6 দুই Subject and দিয়ে যুক্ত হলে সাধারণত Plural Verb
Rahim and Karim are friends.
The teacher and the student have arrived.
7 একটি ব্যক্তি/ধারণা বোঝালে Singular
Bread and butter is my breakfast.
Slow and steady wins the race.
8 Each + Singular Noun
Each student has a book.
Each boy plays football.
9 Every + Singular Noun
Every student is present.
Every boy has a pen.
10 Each of / Every one of + Plural Noun → Singular
Each of the students is present.
Every one of the boys has a book.
১১–১৫. Either, Neither ও As well as
11 Either / Neither + Singular Noun
Either boy is suitable.
Neither boy is present.
12 Either...or → কাছের Subject
Either Rahim or Karim is responsible.
Either Rahim or his friends are responsible.
13 Neither...nor → কাছের Subject
Neither the students nor the teacher is present.
Neither the teacher nor the students are present.
14 Not only...but also → কাছের Subject
Not only Rahim but also his friends are coming.
15 As well as / Along with / Together with
Rahim, as well as his friends, is present.
The teacher, along with the students, is going.
১৬–২০. Collective ও Uncountable Noun
16 Collective Noun → Unit হলে Singular
The team is strong.
The committee has decided.
17 Members আলাদা বোঝালে Plural হতে পারে
The team are arguing among themselves.
18 Uncountable Noun → Singular
The information is useful.
The furniture is expensive.
19 News → Singular
The news is true.
20 Mathematics / Physics / Economics → Singular
Mathematics is difficult.
Physics is interesting.
২১–২৫. Pair, Number ও One
21 Scissors / Trousers / Pants → Plural
The scissors are sharp.
My trousers are new.
22 A pair of → Singular
A pair of scissors is on the table.
23 A number of + Plural → Plural Verb
A number of students are absent.
24 The number of + Plural → Singular Verb
The number of students is increasing.
25 One of + Plural Noun → Singular Verb
One of my friends is a doctor.
২৬–৩০. Special Number Rules
26 More than one → Singular
More than one student has failed.
27 Many a + Singular → Singular
Many a student has failed.
28 One or two + Plural → Plural
One or two students are absent.
29 A lot of / Lots of → পরের Noun অনুযায়ী
A lot of students are present.
A lot of water is wasted.
30 Some / Most / All / Half of
Some of the students are absent.
Some of the water is dirty.
৩১–৩৫. Percentage, Time ও There
31 Percentage / Fraction
Fifty percent of the students are present.
Fifty percent of the water is polluted.
32 Time / Money / Distance → একক হিসেবে Singular
Ten years is a long time.
Five thousand taka is enough.
33 There + be → পরের Subject অনুযায়ী
There is a book on the table.
There are many books on the table.
34 Here + be
Here is your book.
Here are your books.
35 No + Noun
No student is absent.
No students are absent.
৩৬–৪০. Relative, Gerund ও Infinitive
36 Who / Which / That → Antecedent অনুযায়ী
The boy who plays football is my friend.
The boys who play football are my friends.
37 One of + Plural + who → Plural
He is one of the students who are honest.
38 The only one + who → Singular
He is the only one of the students who is honest.
39 Gerund as Subject → Singular
Swimming is good exercise.
Reading books is a good habit.
40 Infinitive as Subject → Singular
To walk in the morning is good.
To tell a lie is a sin.
৪১–৪৫. বিশেষ Noun
41 Police / Cattle / People → Plural
The police are investigating.
Cattle are grazing.
People are waiting.
42 People বনাম Person
One person is here.
Many people are here.
43 The + Adjective → একটি শ্রেণি বোঝালে Plural
The rich are not always happy.
The poor need help.
44 Title of Book / Film → Singular
The Arabian Nights is an interesting book.
45 Country / Organization name → Singular
The United States is a developed country.
The United Nations is an international organization.
